What is Lakadong
Turmeric Powder?

Lakadong Turmeric is a high-curcumin variety of turmeric grown in Meghalaya, known for its bright color, strong flavor, and exceptional medicinal properties. It contains up to 12% curcumin — compared to the 2–3% found in regular turmeric — making it significantly more potent.

Sourced directly from the Seven Sisters region of Northeast India, each batch is stone-ground to preserve its natural oils and active compounds.

Storing Your
Turmeric Right

1

Airtight Container

Store in an airtight glass or ceramic container to prevent moisture and oxidation.

2

Cool & Dry Place

Keep away from heat sources. A pantry or kitchen cabinet away from the stove is ideal.

3

Away from Sunlight

Direct sunlight degrades curcumin. Store in a dark spot to maintain potency and vibrant color.

4

Shelf Life

Best consumed within 12 months of opening. Properly stored, it retains full potency for up to 2 years.

Ways to Use
Lakadong Turmeric

01

Golden Milk

Add ½ tsp to warm milk with black pepper and honey for a classic anti-inflammatory drink.

02

Cooking

Use in curries, dals, rice, and soups. A little goes a long way — start with ¼ tsp per serving.

03

Herbal Tea

Steep ½ tsp in hot water with ginger and lemon for a powerful morning wellness ritual.

04

Smoothies

Blend into smoothies or juices for a nutritious, anti-inflammatory boost without altering taste much.

*

Pro tip: Always pair turmeric with black pepper (piperine) — it increases curcumin absorption by up to 2000%.
